CHANGE DOESN'T BEGIN WITH A FEELING

It begins with
one small action.

Breakthrough is a 40-day practice in doing the next right thing before your mood, confidence or circumstances give you permission.

01

Notice

See the thought, pattern or excuse without becoming it.

02

Choose

Separate what you feel from what you actually want to do.

03

Move

Take one concrete action small enough to do today.

04

Repeat

Forty days turns isolated effort into a new way of living.
